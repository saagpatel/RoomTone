# Privacy Policy — Room Tone

Room Tone uses the camera and LiDAR through ARKit to estimate room geometry. It does not use the microphone and does not capture or store photos or video.

Geometry processing and audio synthesis happen on the device. Recordings contain only audio synthesized by the app. They are written temporarily on the device and leave the app only when you explicitly choose a destination in the system share sheet.

Room Tone does not transmit data to the developer or third parties and contains no analytics, advertising, account, or tracking service.

No analytics, no tracking, no third-party services.
