# Room Tone Privacy Policy

Room Tone uses the camera and LiDAR through ARKit to estimate room geometry. It does not use the microphone or save photos or video.

Geometry processing and audio synthesis happen on the device. The recording control writes app mixer output to temporary files on the device; it does not record your surroundings. A recording file leaves the app when you choose a destination in the system share sheet. That destination may use a network and has its own privacy practices.

Room Tone has no backend, account, analytics, advertising, or tracking service. It does not send geometry or camera images to the developer.

The Support and Privacy Policy links open GitHub web pages. Visiting those pages uses a network and is subject to the website's privacy practices.
